How the estimate is calculated

kWh = watts × hours ÷ 1,000
Cost = kWh × your electricity price per kWh

Use the wattage on the product label or manual, and the per-kWh price on your utility bill. If an appliance cycles on and off, uses different power settings, or has standby consumption, actual usage may differ from this simple estimate.

What to check before comparing appliances

  • Use the rated wattage for the specific model and setting you expect to use.
  • Enter your own utility rate and typical daily usage.
  • Compare estimates using the same hours, rate, and time period.

This tool does not retrieve product specifications or electricity rates. Verify both from the appliance documentation and your utility bill.
